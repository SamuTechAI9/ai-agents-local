# Privacy Policy

**Local AI Agents** is a personal, self-hosted automation project. It is not a
commercial service and has no users other than its author.

## What data is accessed

The application uses Google OAuth to read the author's own Google Calendar
events, Gmail messages and Google Sheets. It accesses no other account.

## How the data is used

Data is read at runtime to generate a personal briefing. Processing happens on
the author's own machine. Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third
parties.

## Storage

Notes the author sends to the application are stored in the author's own
Google Sheet. No other data is retained.
